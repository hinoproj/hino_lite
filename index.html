Settings

Theme

Dark and Light are the same layout in two palettes. Kids is warmer and cheers a little louder.

Backup

Your words, sentences, kanji list and scores live on this device only. Export a copy now and then — it is the only way back after clearing the browser or moving to a new device.

Storage

Words and kanji reviews

Each card shows one side; the big button uncovers the rest step by step, and “Show all” uncovers everything at once. Then you rate yourself: Didn't know, Guessed, Knew it or Easy!. Honest ratings decide how soon a card comes back. Each direction keeps its own progress.

A run holds at most 5 new cards (the newest first), a few you already know so they stay fresh, and the cards you are still learning.

Kana scoring

Multiple choice: correct within 3s +5, within 5s +3, slower +1, wrong −5.
Typing: correct +3, wrong −5.
Scores never drop below 0. Learned at 10, confident at 25, counted separately for each mode.

Kana mistakes

Pick a wrong option and it comes back as one of the choices the next time that kana is asked. Each kana remembers its last 3 wrong picks, separately for Read and Recall; a newer mistake pushes out the oldest. Typing is not affected.

Reset

Erases all words and sentences, your kanji list and every score. Theme and run length stay. There is no undo — export first.
